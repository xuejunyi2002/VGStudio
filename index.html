KAIJUCORP

Be the Kaiju. Beat the Kaiju.

Take control of a city-leveling kaiju, or gear up as the strike team sent to bring it down. KAIJU CORP is an asymmetric action brawler where scale is the weapon — every building is cover, every street is a battlefield, and every match ends with someone's HQ in ruins.
