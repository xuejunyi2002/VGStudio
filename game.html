THE PITCH

One kaiju. Four hunters. One city left standing — maybe.

KAIJU CORP throws five players into an asymmetric standoff: one takes control of a towering kaiju built to level skyscrapers with a swipe, while the other four suit up as an elite strike team armed with experimental military hardware. Every match is a race — can the hunters bring the beast down before it turns the map into rubble?
