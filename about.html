About

We're a bunch of people brought together by one thing: our love for games!

What started as a shared passion turned into VGS Studio, a small indie team of four developers, artists, and creators working together to bring our ideas to life.

Along the way, we've been lucky to have an amazing group of volunteer artists and creative contributors join us, bringing their own talents, ideas, and creativity to the world of KAIJU CORP.

Contact us at vgs.studio.ca@gmail.com
